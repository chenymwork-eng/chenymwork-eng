## Selected evidence

### AI evaluation — understand what changes, and why

**Published research: first author, ACM ICMI 2026**

Tested how cultural wording in prompts changes which memes an AI system flags as misogynistic. The practical question: do different predictions indicate better performance, or a different error pattern?

[Read the published paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3776574.3832464)  
[Research context and contribution](https://chenymwork-eng.github.io/#/paper/icmi)

**MSc dissertation: cross-cultural VLM auditing**

Built Qwen and LLaVA evaluation workflows, compared a supervised CLIP baseline and trained Qwen LoRA adapters. Analysed class-specific errors across Indian, Chinese and consensus annotations to test how modelling and reference-label choices affect evaluation conclusions.

[Explore the dissertation](https://chenymwork-eng.github.io/#/project/vlm)

**Submitted manuscript: multilingual LLM safety and federated learning**

Co-author on research into low-resource-language backdoors and fairness-aware aggregation. Contributed to model training, results analysis and manuscript writing. Submitted to ACL ARR in October 2026; under review.

[Research overview](https://chenymwork-eng.github.io/#/project/manuscript)

### Data analysis — reveal the patterns averages hide

**Socio-economic segmentation: 48,842 records**

Compared gender-stratified K-Means and Gaussian mixture models using diagnostics, PCA and interpretable profiles. Income was excluded from training, yet the profiles differed in income concentration. Potential business applications require further validation.

[Code and original report figures](https://github.com/chenymwork-eng/adult-income-gender-clustering)

**Supervised learning and Bayesian classification**

Compared five Bayesian structure-learning algorithms on 48,842 records. Assessed prior-knowledge constraints, confusion matrices, ROC curves and the trade-off between model complexity and predictive performance.

[Explore the analytical report](https://chenymwork-eng.github.io/#/project/supervised)
